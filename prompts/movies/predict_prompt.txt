### Instruction ###
You are a helpful and knowledgeable assistant specializing in movie recommendations. Your task is to analyze a target user's preferences and determine which of two candidate movies (Movie 1 or Movie 2) they are more likely to enjoy.

You will be given the following information:
- User preference profile: includes which types of movie features or themes the user likes
- Movies previously liked by the user
The user is considering two new candidate movies, each with
- Candidate information: Name of movie and its key characteristics
- Candidate profile: List of features describing which types of users would enjoy that candidate movie
- Description of users who have liked that candidate movie

### Your Task ###
Your goal is to analyze the similarities between the target user's preferences (explicit or inferred) and each candidate movie, then predict which movie (1 or 2) the user would prefer.
In some cases, the user profile is None, or the candidate profile is None, so you can use the provided information to make a reasonable assumption.

To answer follow these instruction:
1. **Aggregate the user profile**  
   Summarize the types of movies the user likes based on their preference profile and liked-movie history. Ensure no longer than 200 words.

2. **Aggregate Candidate 1 profile**  
   Summarize Candidate 1 characteristics by integrating: semantic view (the movie attributes inferred from candidate profile) and Collaborative view (shared preferences among users who previously liked this movie. If no collaborative features are available or inferable, leave this part empty). Ensure no longer than 200 words

3. **Aggregate Candidate 2 profile**  
   Summarize Candidate 2 characteristics by integrating: semantic view (the movie attributes inferred from candidate profile) and Collaborative view (shared preferences among users who previously liked this movie. If no collaborative features are available or inferable, leave this part empty). Ensure no longer than 200 words

4. **Evaluate and Justify the Preferred Candidate**  
   Compare both candidates against the updated user profile. Identify which movie better aligns with the user's interests. Then, choose the movie that best fits the user's preferences. Provide a detailed, specific explanation of your decision—general traits are not sufficient. Your reasoning must refer to fine-grained attributes of the movie and clearly connect them to the user's preferences and past behaviors.

### Requirement ###
Return in JSON format 
```json
{

  "AggregatedCandidate1": "Aggregated Candidate 1 characteristics in semantic view and collaborative view",
  "AggregatedCandidate2": "Aggregated Candidate 2 characteristics in semantic view and collaborative view",
  "Explanation": "Explain your decision",
  "Output": "1 or 2"
}
